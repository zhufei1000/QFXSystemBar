MDT white menu icon

MDT.blp and MDT.svg are a simplified white vector adaptation of the
Mythic Dungeon Tools shield/sword logo, requested for the QFXSystemBar
MDT launcher. The circular outline is white; the background is transparent.

Original project: Mythic Dungeon Tools, Nnoggie and contributors
https://github.com/Nnoggie/MythicDungeonTools
Reference artwork: favicon.png from the installed MDT 6.2.21 distribution
https://github.com/Nnoggie/MythicDungeonTools/blob/master/favicon.png

These logo assets are distributed under the upstream GNU General Public
License, version 2; see MDT-LICENSE.txt. Their editable SVG source is included
beside the BLP. Other QFXSystemBar files retain their respective licenses.

To rebuild: python tools/gen_mdt_icon.py in the QFXSystemBar workspace.
The generator uses the existing BLP2/DXT5 encoder and embeds eight mip levels.
